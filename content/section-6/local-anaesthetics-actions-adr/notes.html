Chapter 27 · Actions and adverse drug reactions

Actions and ADRs

Every LA is eventually absorbed. Blood level decides the CNS and heart effects.

Local actions

  • Little or no local irritation (clinical LAs)
  • Block non-selectively: sensory endings, nerve trunks, neuromuscular junction, ganglionic synapse, receptors
  • Myelinated fibres more susceptible · small fibres > large
  • ↓ ACh release from motor nerve endings
  • Around a mixed nerve: skin anaesthesia + paralysis of the muscles it supplies

Systemic actions: CNS

Basic action
All LAs = neuronal inhibition
Why stimulation first
Inhibitory neurones are inhibited first
High dose
All neurones inhibited → flat EEG
Synthetic LAs
Much less CNS-active than cocaine · little effect at safe doses · symptoms of overdose: see ADRs below

Pharmacokinetics

  • LAs act where they are put, so kinetics don't decide efficacy
  • Kinetics decide systemic toxicity
Absorption
  • Soluble surface LAs (lidocaine, tetracaine): fast from mucosa and abraded skin; minimal from intact skin
  • Procaine: doesn't cross mucous membranes
  • More blood flow, more absorption: intercostal > sciatic block
↓
Distribution
  • Lipophilic → brain, heart, liver, kidney first, then muscle
  • Procaine: negligible protein binding · amides: α1-acid glycoprotein
  • Briefly bound to nerves at the injection site
↓

  • Oral procaine and lidocaine: high first-pass → useless orally as antiarrhythmics

Precautions and interactions

1. Aspirate lightly before injecting → avoid intravascular injection
↓
2. Inject slowly; don't exceed the max safe dose, especially in children
↓
3. Propranolol (likely other β-blockers) ↓ hepatic blood flow → ↓ metabolism of lidocaine and other amides
↓
4. Avoid adrenaline-containing LA in the patients below
Ischaemic heart disease, Cardiac arrhythmia, Thyrotoxicosis, Uncontrolled hypertension, On β-blockers, On tricyclic antidepressants
  • β-blockers: unopposed α action → rise in BP
  • Tricyclics: block adrenaline uptake → potentiate it
For your clinicAspirate before every block. For a patient with heart disease, uncontrolled hypertension, thyrotoxicosis, or on propranolol or amitriptyline, use a cartridge without adrenaline.
